The Jordan Brand

The Nike Jordan Brand is one of the greatest successes in sports marketing history. Starting in 1984, Nike partnered with Michael Jordan to create a signature line, establishing the brand as one of the industry's dominant names.

The brand released its first shoe in 1985 by introducing the Air Jordan I. Jordan created massive consumer demand and a cultural phenomenon. They successfully expanded the line in subsequent years by releasing new annual models, with each design delivering several memorable innovations.

In 1997, Nike completed the transition by creating a distinct subsidiary. The brand grew to generate billions of dollars in annual revenue and won its place as a permanent staple of street fashion.

The Jordan Brand helped establish the Jumpman logo as one of the most important symbols of its generation. Its success with consumers also helped increase the popularity of sneaker culture around the world.
