Michael Jordan

The First 3-Peat

Michael Jordan led the Chicago Bulls to three consecutive NBA championships from 1991 to 1993. During this period, he established himself as the dominant player in basketball and won three NBA Finals MVP awards. This run marked the beginning of the Bulls dynasty.

The Jordan Brand

In 1984, Michael Jordan signed with Nike, creating one of the most successful athlete partnerships in history. The Air Jordan shoe line and the Jumpman logo became global symbols of basketball culture. Today, Jordan Brand is one of the most recognizable sports brands in the world.

The Last Shot

On June 14, 1998, Jordan scored the game-winning basket against the Utah Jazz in Game 6 of the NBA Finals. The shot secured the Bulls' sixth championship and completed their second three-peat. It remains one of the most famous moments in sports history.
