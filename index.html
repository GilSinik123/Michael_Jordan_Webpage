Michael Jordan

The First 3-Peat

Michael Jordan led the Chicago Bulls to three consecutive NBA Championships from 1991 to 1993, defeating the Los Angeles Lakers, Portland Trail Blazers, and Phoenix Suns. This historic achievement established Jordan and the Bulls as basketball's dominant team of the early 1990s.

Did you know? Michael Jordan won all three NBA Finals MVP awards during the Bulls' first three-peat?

Some More Cool Facts

  1. Michael Jordan was cut from his high school varsity basketball team as a sophomore, motivating him to work even harder.
  2. Jordan briefly retired from basketball in 1993 and played professional baseball for the Birmingham Barons, a minor league team.
  3. He wore the jersey number 45 when he returned to the NBA in 1995 before switching back to his famous number 23.
  4. Michael Jordan played his final two NBA seasons with the Washington Wizards from 2001 to 2003.
  5. Jordan won the NBA Defensive Player of the Year Award in 1988, showing he was an elite defender as well as a scorer.
  6. Jordan was known for practising harder than many of his teammates, setting an example with his dedication and competitive attitude.
