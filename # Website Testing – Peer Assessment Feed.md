# Website Testing – Peer Assessment Feedback

**Website:** [Michael Jordan Website](https://gilsinik123.github.io/Michael_Jordan_Webpage/)

**Reviewers:** Lailai, my younger brother and my younger sister.

---

# Sprint 1 – Initial Website

## 1. Aesthetics – How does it look?

**Contrast:** The headings and text were clear and easy to read.
**Alignment:** The text and images needed better alignment.
**Proximity:** Some images needed to be positioned closer to the information they described.
**Consistency:** The secondary page needed to match the main page more closely.
**Balance:** The layout could be more organised.
**White Space:** The spacing between text and images could be improved.

**Score (0–6): 4/6**

**Feedback:**

**Positive:** I liked the clear headings and relevant images, which made the website interesting and easy to follow.

**Suggestion:** I would make the fonts, colours and spacing more consistent and position each image beside the information it describes.

## 2. Functionality – Does it work?

**Links:** Navigation between the pages could be improved.
**Navigation:** A Back button was needed to return to the main page.
**Scrolling:** The page was straightforward to scroll through.
**Page Load:** The images needed to be checked to ensure they displayed correctly.
**Responsive:** The layout needed to be checked on different screen sizes.

**Score (0–5): 3/5**

**Feedback:**

**Positive:** I found the secondary page easy to scroll through and view.

**Suggestion:** I would add a visible Back button and check that the images and navigation worked correctly.

## 3. Usability – Is it easy to use?

**Readability:** The paragraphs and headings made the information easy to read.
**Accessibility:** Clear headings helped users understand the content.
**Find Info:** The information was understandable, but the connection between text and images could be clearer.
**User Friendly:** The page was straightforward to explore.

**Score (0–4): 3/4**

**Feedback:**

**Positive:** I found the information easy to understand because it was divided into paragraphs with clear headings.

**Suggestion:** I would place each paragraph beside the image it describes, such as putting the shoe information beside the shoe image.

## 4. Bonus Criteria (Optional)

**Purpose:** The website presented Michael Jordan's career and brand.
**Engaging:** The relevant images made the content more interesting.
**Spelling:** The text was understandable.

**Score (0–3): 2/3**

**Feedback:**

**Positive:** I liked how the images made the information about the Jordan Brand and Michael Jordan's achievements more interesting.

**Suggestion:** I would make the connection between the images and information clearer and make the secondary page more consistent with the main website.

**Total: 12/18**
